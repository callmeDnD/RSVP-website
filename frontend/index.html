        Frequently asked questions

        Is the event free?

        Registration is for planning only. Check the schedule and announcements for any fees or updates.

        Can I bring a guest?

        Yes. Enter the total number of people, including yourself, in the RSVP form.

        How do I change or cancel my RSVP?

        Use the link in your confirmation email, or go to the manage page and enter your reference number and email.

        I did not get a confirmation email.

        Check your spam folder. If it is not there, contact the organizers with the email you used to register.

        Will the event be streamed?

        See the live streaming section. The link is posted there before the event starts.

        What should I bring?

        Bring a valid ID and your reference number. See the venue section for details.
